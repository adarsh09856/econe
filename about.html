Our Vision

Empowering Bhutanese Commerce with Direct Factory Access

Historically, businesses in Bhutan faced prohibitive logistics costs, minimum order requirements, and complex customs documentation when trying to import goods from overseas. Bhutan Global Mart was established in Thimphu to transform this reality.

Through our automated AI shopping platform and established logistics conduits through Kolkata port and Phuentsholing dry port, we pool wholesale orders across Bhutan to achieve rock-bottom factory-tier pricing on Alibaba, AliExpress, and Amazon.
